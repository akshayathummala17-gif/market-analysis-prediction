Model Architecture & Anti-Leakage Pipeline

Linear Regression

Fits an optimal parametric hyperplane mapping price differentials, competitor ratios, and seasonal factors. Serves as our transparent, highly interpretable baseline.

Random Forest Regressor

Ensemble of 100 decorrelated decision trees with bootstrap aggregation. Accurately captures non-linear price elasticity curves and high-order category interactions without overfitting.

Decision Tree Regressor

Constructs recursive binary decision splits up to depth 8. Ideal for segmenting products into performance tiers and identifying pricing threshold breakpoints.
